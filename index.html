The Pho Vietnam 3969 Restaurant

Pho Vietnam 3969 has been serving Niagara Falls from 3969 Montrose Road since 2014, and has grown into one of the region's go-to spots for Vietnamese comfort food — known for generous portions, a huge menu, and a warm, family-friendly dining room.

Our menu spans the full range of Vietnamese home cooking — pho, vermicelli bowls, bánh mì, cơm tấm — alongside Chinese-style fried rice, chow mein, and hot pot for the table, plus a full vegetarian and vegan section. Whatever the craving, there's a bowl for it.

Stop in for dine-in, reservations, or call ahead for takeout — free parking makes it easy, just minutes from the Falls.
