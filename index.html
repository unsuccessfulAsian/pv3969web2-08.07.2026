Our Story

Pho Vietnam 3969 was born from a love of the bowls that bring families together. We blend traditional Vietnamese cooking with beloved Chinese classics, honoring recipes passed down through generations while bringing fresh, local ingredients to every table.

Our pho is our pride: bones simmered low and slow, charred aromatics, and a broth that takes all day to get right. Pull up a seat and taste the difference a family recipe makes.
